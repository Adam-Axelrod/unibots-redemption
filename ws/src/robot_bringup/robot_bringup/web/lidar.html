Checking orientation

Hold a hand about 30 cm in front of the robot. The red nearest dot should appear straight above the robot, near 0°.

Then move to its left: the dot should move to the left, near +90°. If it shows up somewhere else, the lidar is rotated by that angle. If left and right are swapped, it is upside down or its angle direction is reversed.

Hover over any dot to read its angle and range.
